What we check, and why

  • The registry number on your document against the profile
  • The legal name against the profile
  • A registry lookup where one is possible
  • Your email domain against the company website
  • Whether the document is internally consistent

A strong match is approved immediately. Anything short of that goes to a person. We would rather make you wait a day than let somebody publish credentials in your company's name.

A rejected claim can be retried once with better evidence.
