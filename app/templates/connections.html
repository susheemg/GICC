What the platform can and cannot do in your drive

  • Read only. It never edits, moves, renames or deletes your files.
  • One folder. It reads the folder you choose and its sub-folders.
  • Nothing new publishes itself. A first-seen file lands as a draft. Once you confirm it, later revisions of the same file publish as renewals.
  • Deleting a file in your drive deletes nothing here. You get an action instead, because a buyer may be relying on that credential.
  • Disconnecting deletes the access tokens and keeps everything already filed.
